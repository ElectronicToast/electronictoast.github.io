Electromagnetic inverse design typically requires repeated simulations of an entire device, even when each optimization step changes only a small part of the geometry. This work introduces the **Precomputed Numerical Green Function (PNGF) method** to reduce the cost of evaluating candidate designs.

The unchanged surroundings are encoded in a numerical Green function matrix, leaving a linear system whose unknowns cover **only the region being optimized**. Combined with direct binary search, the method makes objective-function evaluation **scale linearly with the optimization region size**. This approach enables rapid design without a previously generated simulation dataset or a trained surrogate model.
