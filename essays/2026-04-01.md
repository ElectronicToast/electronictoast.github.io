<!--- split -->

Training deep-learning surrogate models for electromagnetic inverse design requires large datasets that are expensive to generate with conventional full-wave solvers. This work combines a **Precomputed Numerical Green Function (PNGF) solver** with **direct binary search** to rapidly produce high-quality, large training datasets within a fixed computational budget.

A proof-of-concept deep-learning model predicts the performance of pixelated metallic antennas on dielectric substrates. The training dataset, with **six million full-wave simulations**, was generated **more than 3,600 times faster** compared to the time required with Ansys HFSS. Because the simulations include the substrate directly, the model does not require transfer learning from free-space data, unlike prior approaches.
