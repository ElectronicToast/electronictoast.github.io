<!--- split -->

Magnetic nanoparticle labels are a promising approach for compact, inexpensive, yet highly-sensitive diagnostic assays. In oscillator-based magnetic biosensors, however, temperature changes and other slow disturbances can shift the sensing frequency and obscure the response of the magnetic labels.

This work presents a drift-compensation approach based on **concurrent dual-frequency oscillators**. The two concurrent sensing frequencies allow drift to be tracked during the measurement. The approach supports frequency-sweep spectroscopy and **single-step, wash-free assays** in a standard CMOS package, enabling low-power magnetic biosensing for practical point-of-care diagnostics.
