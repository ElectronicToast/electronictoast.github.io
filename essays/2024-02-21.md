Electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR) is a powerful spectroscopic technique for **direct detection and characterization of free radicals**, with high sensitivity and specificity suited for a wide range of uses, including biomedical applications such as site-directed spin labeling and potential applications in quantum technology such as spin qubit control. 

In this work, a **14GHz spectrometer IC** is presented that realizes, for the first time, pulse EPR spectroscopy on a highly integrated and low-power platform with **on-chip multi-pulse sequencing**. A **direct-conversion subharmonic architecture** is introduced, enabling a voltage-controlled oscillator to be used as a flexible on-chip sensing cell and providing direct detection of the transverse magnetization after pulses.
