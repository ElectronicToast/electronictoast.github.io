<!--- split -->

This work presents an integrated electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR) spectrometer supporting both pulse and continuous-wave (CW) measurements. A **subharmonic direct-conversion receiver** uses a reference oscillator at half the sensing frequency to generate excitation pulses through injection locking and to provide the local oscillator to recover the spin response after each pulse.

The 65nm CMOS prototype contains **two independent sensing cells** and is integrated into a portable system that is powered and controlled through USB. Experiments include free induction decay, inversion recovery, Hahn echoes, stimulated echoes, and CW spectroscopy. The demonstrated tuning range of **12.8-14.9GHz** and **CW sensor power as low as 2.1mW** highlight its suitability for compact yet highly-sensitive EPR-based sensors.
