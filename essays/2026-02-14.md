<!--- split -->

The **Precomputed Numerical Green Function (PNGF) method** accelerates electromagnetic inverse design by separating the region being optimized from the unchanged static environment. A single precomputation captures the background interactions, and low-rank matrix updates allow candidate geometries to be **evaluated in milliseconds** while retaining the accuracy of the underlying full-wave solver.

The method is demonstrated on a broadband substrate antenna, a reconfigurable switched-beam antenna, and a compact microstrip-to-substrate-integrated-waveguide transition. Fabricated devices validate the predicted responses. The PNGF technique reaches speeds up to **16,000 times** faster compared to conventional solver-based optimization, bringing designs that previously required days or weeks to synthesize within reach on much shorter timescales without the training required for deep-learning models.
